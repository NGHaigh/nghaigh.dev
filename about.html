About

Hi there I'm Nathan Haigh, an aspiring Game Developer from Exeter England. I am currently a Student at UWE (University of the West of England) based in Bristol England. To make it long story short, I love Games. From the techical side, to the creative side and everything inbetween. Games are the most facinating and diverse forms of software I have ever seen. The more I play games, the more i want i be involved in their production, so I can create something that has as big as an impact as games have had on me.

When I'm not working on my projects, I can be found playing games or following up on my favourite football team (Chelsea, biggest club in London), tinkering with 3D printing and 3D modelling and balancing screens with running and working in my garden.

I am always looking for any oppurtunities to break into the Games Industry, so if you have any questions or would like to get in touch, please feel free to contact me via the contact page.
